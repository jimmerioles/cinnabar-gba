What you are hearing

The four Game Boy channels and both DMA sound FIFOs, mixed to 48 kHz stereo and resampled to your audio device. Audio starts after one tap because browsers require a gesture.

What you are seeing

Modes 0–5, text and affine backgrounds, sprites, windows, mosaic, alpha blending and brightness effects, rendered scanline by scanline and presented through WebGL.

What is not here

No BIOS image, no link cable, no netplay, and no ROMs. The emulator uses its own HLE BIOS and ships only original demo cartridges. Bring your own legally dumped game.

Cinnabar GBA is an independent clean-room emulator. Game Boy Advance is a trademark of Nintendo; this project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo.
